Arc Desk Lamp: 40-Second Product Demo

A faceless product video in a Problem → Product → Proof → Offer format, for a brand's YouTube, reels and product page. Five 8-second clips, no presenter, hands only, with a voice-over.

Format: product demo 40 s · 16:9, with a 9:16 cut 5 clips × 8 s AI voice-over, no on-camera host

Sample · AI-assistedConcept work by ASTRALX Studio to show the service, not client work. "Lumaro" and "Arc" are made-up names; the product and its features are invented. Clips are made with AI video generation and the voice-over is AI-generated; script, direction and edit are by a person.
